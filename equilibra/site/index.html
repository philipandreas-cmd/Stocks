Cost of understocking

Watch

$6,240

Expected lost margin · 24% chance of a stockout

Margin you are likely to lose from customers who can't buy, weighted by how probable each demand level is. The probabilities come from a Bayesian update: your prior forecast is revised as real sales and pre-orders arrive.

P(demand | sales) ∝ P(sales | demand) · P(demand)
E[cost] = (price − cost) · E[(D − Q)⁺]

What to do: stockout risk is concentrated in the hero SKU (50ml serum). An air top-up of 600 units cuts this to $2,900.

Low

Cash you are likely to lose on units that don't sell at full price: the markdown to clear them plus 3PL storage while they wait. Uses the same posterior demand curve as the understock card, so the two always trade off honestly.

E[cost] = (cost − clearance + holding) · E[(Q − D)⁺]

What to do: nothing this cycle. Leftover risk is small next to stockout risk.

How it works

One monthly planning cycle, done in an afternoon

Integrated business planning is what larger brands run with a team of analysts. Equilibra runs the same cycle for a team of two.

  1. Connect

    Link Shopify or WooCommerce, your ad accounts and your 3PL. Supplier lead times and costs come from a short form.

  2. Forecast

    Demand per SKU with seasonality, promos and planned ad spend. The forecast updates as each week of sales arrives.

  3. Balance

    Pick a demand target. Equilibra sets the order quantity, the marketing budget by channel and the cash you need.

  4. Ship

    Choose freight modes and split quantities by total landed cost and stockout risk, then export POs.
